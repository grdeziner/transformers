Optimus Prime

Alt mode: Red cab-over semi-truck

“Freedom is the right of all sentient beings.”

The largest, strongest and wisest of all Autobots. Optimus Prime leads the Autobots in their war against Megatron's Decepticons, a war that crashed onto prehistoric Earth aboard the Ark and woke up again in 1984.

He sees his role as the protector of all life, Earth-life included. His toy-box bio said that if he'd been born on Earth he would have been a doctor, a mechanic, a scientist and a warrior. On Cybertron there's no difference between those jobs. His one flaw? He cares too much about everyone around him.

  • Weapon Laser rifle & energy axe
  • Trailer Combat Deck with Roller
  • Catchphrase “Autobots, transform and roll out!”

    Autobot · Espionage

    Bumblebee

    Alt mode: Yellow Volkswagen Beetle

    “The least likely can be the most dangerous.”

    Small, eager and brave, Bumblebee is the Autobots' messenger and spy. He's small enough to go where the big guys can't, and he doesn't look like a threat, so the Decepticons tend to overlook him. In the cartoon he's the first Autobot to make friends with Spike Witwicky.

    He looks up to the bigger Autobots, especially Optimus Prime and Prowl, and he badly wants to be accepted. He's the most fuel-efficient Autobot, he has the sharpest eyes of the bunch, and he can work underwater. He's physically the weakest Autobot, but he makes up for it with stealth.

    • Class Mini-Vehicle (Minibot)
    • Specialty Scouting & underwater recon
    • Best friend Spike Witwicky
